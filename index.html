01 / About the event

Imagine. Innovate.
Inspire.

Where ideas become innovation — bringing curious minds together to explore emerging technologies and practical solutions.

Welcome to IDEAFEST 2026

IDEAFEST 2026 is organized by the ARINDASCI Association of the Department of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at Sir Isaac Newton College of Engineering and Technology. The expo encourages innovation, interdisciplinary collaboration, technical knowledge sharing, and the practical application of emerging technologies.

InnovationCollaborationPractical learningTechnology

Our purpose

Give student innovators an opportunity to demonstrate technical skills, present projects, exchange knowledge, and explore solutions to real-world challenges.
